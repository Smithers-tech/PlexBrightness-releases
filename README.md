# Plex Brightness

Plex Brightness turns your phone's screen brightness up while a video is playing in Plex, then puts it back the way it was when you stop or leave Plex. It runs entirely on your phone: no account, no internet, no ads.

## Download

Get the latest **PlexBrightness-x.x.apk** from the **[Releases](https://github.com/Smithers-tech/PlexBrightness-releases/releases/latest)** page. Open it on your phone and tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.

Requires Android 7 or newer. Under 1 MB.

## Setup (two steps, once)

The app's home screen walks you through these:

1. **Allow brightness changes**: turn on "Allow modifying system settings".
2. **Let it spot Plex videos**: in **Settings → Accessibility**, tap **Plex Brightness** and switch it on.

On Android 13 and newer, step 2 may be greyed out with "Controlled by restricted setting", because the app isn't from the Play Store. To allow it: try switching it on once, then open **App info** for Plex Brightness, tap **⋮** in the top corner, choose **Allow restricted settings**, and do step 2 again.

## What it does

- Brightens the screen only while a Plex video is on screen, and restores your own brightness (including auto-brightness) afterwards.
- Choose how bright videos should be, and tap **Preview** to try a level without leaving the app.
- A switch to pause it, and a status card that shows whether it's ready or brightening now.
- A troubleshooting log shows what it noticed, in case a video doesn't brighten.

## Privacy

The accessibility permission is only used to tell whether Plex is showing a video. The app has no internet permission, so nothing ever leaves your phone.
